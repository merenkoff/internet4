OwnNet Internet 4.0

Відкритий інтернет для всіх

Рух за цифрову власність, протоколи замість платформ, суверенітет над своїми даними та пристроями.

Це не стартап і не токен. Це рух і технічна доктрина: маніфест, архітектурні принципи та публічні кейси для тих, хто хоче інтернет без віддалених вимкнень і замкнених екосистем.

Manifesto of the Open Internet 4.0

  1. Digital Ownership Is Real Ownership. If you buy it, you own it. No remote revocation. No hidden kill switches.
  2. Devices Must Work Without Permission. Hardware must not depend on continuous corporate authorization to function.
  3. Software Is Infrastructure. Critical digital services must rely on open standards and auditable protocols.
  4. Data Belongs to the Individual. User data must be exportable, portable, and locally accessible.
  5. Offline First. Every essential system must degrade gracefully and function without central servers.
  6. Interoperability Is a Right. No artificial lock-ins. No forced ecosystems.
  7. Transparency Over Trust. Trust must be replaced by verifiable architecture.
  8. Repair, Modify, Fork. Users must have the legal and technical right to repair and modify their digital tools.
  9. Protocols Over Platforms. The internet should be built on open protocols, not corporate walled gardens.
  10. Digital Sovereignty Is Civil Sovereignty. Control over digital infrastructure equals political autonomy.
